Classic Creamy Tuscan Garlic Chicken Italian • 30 Mins
Adjust Serving Count:
2

Ingredients List (Scaled):

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions
  1. Season chicken breasts with sea salt, pepper, and dried oregano.
  2. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, heat olive oil and sear chicken 5 mins per side until golden.
  3. Remove chicken; add minced garlic, sundried tomatoes, and fresh spinach to skillet.
  4. Pour heavy cream and simmer for 3 minutes until sauce thickens.
  5. Return chicken to pan, spoon sauce over, and garnish with fresh basil.
