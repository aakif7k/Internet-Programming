Income Tax Calculation Web Application

Exact statutory tax formula implementation: Gross Pay, House Rent Allowance (HRA), Standard Deduction (Rs. 50,000), Pension (capped at Rs. 150,000), Health Insurance, with exact slab calculation.

Employee Financial Details
Max deduction capped at Rs. 1,50,000/yr
Tax Computation Statement
Government Tax Calculation Norms
Net Taxable Income Formula:
Net Taxable Income = (Gross Pay * 12) - (HRA * 12) - 50,000 [Standard Deduction] - min(Pension * 12, 1,50,000) - (Health Insurance * 12)

Tax Slab Rates:

  • If Net Taxable Income ≤ Rs. 5,00,000: Tax is NIL (Rs. 0)
  • If Net Taxable Income > Rs. 5,00,000:
    • 0 to 2,50,000: 0%
    • 2,50,001 to 5,00,000: 5% of (Net - 2,50,000)
    • 5,00,001 to 10,00,000: Rs. 12,500 + 20% of (Net - 5,00,000)
    • Above 10,00,000: Rs. 1,12,500 + 30% of (Net - 10,00,000)
