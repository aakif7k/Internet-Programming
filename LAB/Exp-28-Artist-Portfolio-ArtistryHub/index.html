About the Artist: Emily Harris

Emily Harris is a contemporary visual painter recognized for her atmospheric landscapes and expressive impasto textures. Educated at the Royal Academy of Arts, her work has been showcased in major retrospectives in London, Paris, and Tokyo.

Her creative philosophy explores the fragile harmony between untouched natural landscapes and transient human emotions.
